## Bond study (Study A regression)
* Same conclusions as before: HAD-Stack lowers the worst-case RMSE of the undefended forecaster at ε = 0.5 in all 8 runs
  (all intervals exclude zero; at ε = 1.0 in 8/8, intervals exclude zero in 7/8 — fold 1 now touches zero);
  clean RMSE falls in 7/8; the added error shrinks in 4/8 (0.5) and 2/8 (1.0); fixed-budget AT is more robust in 8/8.
* Budget range over the runs: 0.3686–0.6413 (never reaches the new maximum 0.6428).
* QPSO followers (fixed split seed 1, folds 1–2): loop behaves as before; every Hamiltonian-controlled forecaster lowers
  the joint-attack worst-case RMSE of the undefended one at both budgets in all three runs, intervals exclude zero.
* Figures regenerated: `paper/figs/f_bond_dynamics.pdf`, `f_bond_tau.pdf`, `f_bond_forecast.pdf`, `f_bond_qpso.pdf`.

* Fraud study on the UPI and card data (`RUN_ADAGB.ps1`, on the authors' machine).
